EASY TO START

How to play

Jump into the arena and take turns rolling, moving, and racing your tokens home.

1

Start a match

Open the game board, choose your mode, and pick a 4-minute or 10-minute match.

2

Roll and move

Roll the dice on your turn. When a token can move, tap a highlighted token on the board.

3

Keep your turns

Act before the turn timer runs out. Miss three turns and your player is eliminated.
